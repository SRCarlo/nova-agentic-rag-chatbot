# TechNest Store FAQ

## Return policy

Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. Opened headphones and earbuds can only be returned if defective, for hygiene reasons.

## How to start a return

Log in to your account, open Orders, select the item, and click "Return item". Print the prepaid label and drop the parcel at any courier point. Returns without a label cannot be processed.

## Refund timeline

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. UPI and card refunds may take an additional 2-3 days to appear in your bank statement.

## Shipping times and charges

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders above Rs 999. Orders below Rs 999 have a shipping fee of Rs 49. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs Rs 149.

## International shipping

We currently ship only within India. International shipping is planned for next year.

## Order cancellation

Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped, from Orders > Cancel. Once an order is marked Shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery.

## Warranty

All electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Accessories such as cables and cases have a 6-month warranty. Warranty does not cover physical damage or water damage.

## Payment methods

We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, and Cash on Delivery for orders up to Rs 5000. EMI is available on orders above Rs 3000.

## Damaged or wrong item received

If you received a damaged or incorrect item, contact support within 48 hours of delivery with a photo. We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund.

## Support hours and contact

Customer support is available 9am-6pm, Monday to Saturday. Email: support@technest.example. Phone: 1800-000-1234 (toll free).

## Password and account issues

Use "Forgot password" on the login page to reset your password by email. If you do not receive the email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder or contact support.
